## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Is using mathematical concepts that happen to be similar a violation?

**A:** Independent invention of similar mathematical frameworks is not a violation. However, if your work is directly derived from, inspired by, or implements the specific formulations in this repository, proper attribution is required. Commercial use requires licensing.

### Q: What if we call it something different?

**A:** The Functional Equivalence Principle (see PATENT_NOTICE.md) states that any constant or computational element serving the same purpose as the Bottleneck Constant I, regardless of naming, may be considered an implementation of the same concept. Attribution is still required.

### Q: Can we contribute improvements back?

**A:** Yes! Open source contributions are welcome and remain under MIT license. Commercial implementers are encouraged to contribute non-proprietary improvements back to the community.

### Q: What about patent protection?

**A:** This repository serves as defensive publication establishing prior art. Formal patent applications may be filed separately. Commercial licensees receive clarity on IP status and indemnification provisions where applicable.

### Q: Do we need a license if we're just researching?

**A:** Academic research, even in a corporate research lab, typically falls under academic use provisions. However, if results will be integrated into commercial products, licensing should be discussed early.
