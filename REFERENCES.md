## Notes on Phantom References Removed

The following references from the original list were removed as they could not be verified or had significant attribution errors:

- **Smith, J.D. Non-Diophantine Arithmetics (2020)** - No such standard reference exists. Replaced with Burgin (2010) which is the actual work on non-Diophantine arithmetics.

- **Khrushchev, S.V. (2022) Fundamental Physical Constants** - Could not be verified in the cited journal.

- **Kumar, A. (2014) Factorials of real negative and imaginary numbers** - The DOI 10.1186/2193-1801-3-658 points to an unrelated paper. Gamma function references (Artin, Whittaker & Watson) provide proper coverage of this topic.

- **Miller, A.R. (1952) On the Gamma Function** - Misattributed; the cited DOI belongs to a different author (Mordell).
